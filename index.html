How it works

A streamlined six-step process from requirement to after-sales.

1

Requirement Submission

Buyers describe products, quantities, and destination requirements.

2

AI Analysis

AI parses requirements, identifies specs, and flags compliance needs.

3

Supplier Matching

Verified manufacturers are ranked by fit, capacity, and track record.

4

Quotation

Structured quotes with pricing, lead times, and terms for comparison.

5

Procurement

Order placement, contracts, payments, and logistics coordination.

6

After-sales

Ongoing support, parts, repairs, and warranty management.
